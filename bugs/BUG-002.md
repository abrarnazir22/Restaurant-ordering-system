# BUG-002: Table Reservations Permitted in Past Date and Time

- **ID:** BUG-002
- **Severity:** P0 (Critical / Blocker)
- **Priority:** High
- **Component:** Table Reservations (`src/Routes/reservations.js`)
- **Reported By:** Quality Engineering Lead
- **Status:** Verified Fixed

## 1. Description
The reservation booking endpoint permitted users to submit table reservations for past dates (e.g. yesterday) or past times on today's date (e.g., booking for 9:00 AM when the current time is 2:00 PM).

## 2. Steps to Reproduce
1. Navigate to `/customer` or storefront reservation section.
2. Enter guest details and select a date in the past (e.g. `2025-01-01`) or a past hour today.
3. Submit the reservation request.

## 3. Expected Result
The system rejects the reservation with HTTP 400 Bad Request: `"Reservation date and time must be at least 15 minutes in the future."`

## 4. Actual Result
The reservation was persisted in MongoDB with `status: "pending"`.

## 5. Root Cause
Missing server-side chronological validation in `POST /api/reservations`.

## 6. Fix Applied
Implemented `validateFutureDateTime(date, time)` in `src/utils/validation.js` enforcing a minimum 15-minute future buffer and integrated it into `src/Routes/reservations.js`.

## 7. Verification
Verified via `tests/e2e/reservations.spec.js` (Tests `RES-02` and `RES-03`).
